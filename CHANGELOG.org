#+TITLE: Credit Check Changelog
#+STARTUP: content

* v1.2.1 — September 7, 2026

** Changed

- Labeled pageview figures as article views. They count views of the Wikipedia articles carrying your photos at scan time, so a photo added last week inherits that article's earlier views. Reports, the browser picker, and scan output now say so, and explain that per-photo totals overlap when photos share an article.
- Showed the pageview month in the browser picker, including on phones, and kept large totals from being clipped.

** Fixed

- Excluded Main Page placements from article-view totals, since one day on a Main Page can add millions of views. The placements stay listed, and a photo used only on a Main Page shows as excluded rather than zero. Article views saved by earlier versions stay hidden until you create a new reach report with article views.

* v1.2.0 — September 6, 2026

** Added

- Added ~credit-check report~ and *Create a reach report* in the guided menu, with HTML, JSON, and CSV exports.
- Added dated scan snapshots that preserve previous reviews and identify added or removed photos and article placements between scans with matching settings.
- Added optional Wikipedia article pageviews, monthly totals, and photo rankings. Overall totals count each article once, including articles that contain multiple photos.
- Added a report link and scan-date and change information to the browser picker.

** Changed

- Reduced the mobile picker’s header and control spacing so photos appear earlier on the page.
- Changed pageview fetching to one request at a time by default. Throttling stops further requests while preserving completed results.
- Distinguished missing, failed, and deferred pageview measurements from measured zeroes.
- Kept older saved reviews readable without inventing a scan date or missing Wikidata information.

** Fixed

- Fixed photographer attribution accepting a credit for retouching, cropping, or editing alone. Direct photographer credit and credit traced through a source photograph remain supported.
- Fixed reloaded picker pages showing outdated selections.
- Prevented older tabs and delayed saves from overwriting newer selections.
- Fixed picker CSS overriding hidden controls.
- Fixed Gap Check treating missing artist, license, dimensions, or upload-date information as sufficient for a conclusive ranking. These candidates now stay in the uncertain priority band.
